Direct-Impact Donations

We operate with lean overheads and transparent stewardship. Every rand, or foreign contribution, goes directly to the animals.

Where Your Money Goes

100% Direct Care

🥩 Meat & Nutrition

Fresh game cuts and whole poultry that keep our cats lean, strong and healthy.

🌿 Small Cat Habitats

Natural logs, water pools and high climbing frames for our serval, caracal and ocelot enclosures.

🩺 Veterinary Care

Vaccinations, reproductive assessments, regular parasite treatments and 24/7 wildlife emergency standby.

🏗️ Cheetah Enclosures

Building and expanding spacious camps, plus predator-proof perimeter fencing.
