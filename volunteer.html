Barbosa K&K Volunteer Programme

Live, learn and contribute on the front lines of predator conservation. Every cent raised goes directly to meat, veterinary diets, small-cat habitats and cheetah enclosure construction.

Why We Do It

Our volunteer programme exists to support the intense daily demands of ethical predator re-wilding. By joining us you provide essential hands-on help and financial support that guarantees our cats receive optimal nutrition and habitat care.

To ensure stability for our animals and meaningful training for you, the minimum stay is 1 week.

Volunteers in Action

Real Work, Real Impact

Clearing camps, patrolling fences, cutting grass and keeping the reserve in shape. This is what a week at Barbosa K&K looks like.

A Day in the Life

Daily Working Routine

Choose a season:

Winter Daily Timetable

Feeding time begins promptly at 16:00
07:30 – 09:00Morning enclosure rounds: clearing leftovers, collecting bowls, water inspection and butchery prep.
09:00 – 09:30Tea break and team regroup.
09:30 – 13:00Camp maintenance, game fence inspections and invasive alien plant removal.
13:00 – 14:00Lunch break at the guest house.
14:00 – 17:00Afternoon tasks. Final meat prep begins; predator feeding takes place at 16:00.

Summer Daily Timetable

Adjusted to avoid peak heat; cats eat at 17:00 when temperatures drop.
07:30 – 09:00Cool-morning enclosure cleaning, sanitising bowls and butchery prep.
09:00 – 09:30Tea break and shade rest.
09:30 – 13:00Field maintenance and reserve duties before the midday heat.
13:00 – 15:00Extended midday break to rest and stay out of the hot summer sun.
15:00 – 18:00Late-afternoon shift. Feeding prep starts; predator feeding takes place at 17:00.

* Volunteers work 7 days a week, with time off on Saturdays from 12:00 until feeding time, and on Sunday afternoons after the morning rounds.

Good to Know

Volunteer FAQ

What is the minimum stay?

One week, for every programme. This gives the animals stability and gives you enough time to learn the routine properly.

I live nearby. Do I have to stay on the reserve?

No. If you live close enough to drive in and out every day, join as a North West volunteer. You commit to at least one week, pay in advance and bring your own lunch. Contact us for the current fee.

What will I actually be doing?

Morning enclosure rounds, cleaning and refilling water, butchery and meat prep, camp and fence maintenance, removing invasive plants, and helping at predator feeding every afternoon.

Do I get time off?

Volunteers work 7 days a week, with Saturdays off from 12:00 until feeding time and Sunday afternoons off after the morning rounds.

Where will I stay?

South African and international volunteers stay in the reserve guest house; for international volunteers, groceries/meals are included in the fee. North West volunteers commute daily.

What insurance do I need?

South African volunteers need valid South African medical aid. International volunteers need international travel and medical insurance.

Will I be able to touch the cats?

Our zero-exploitation policy means the focus is on care, not contact. You'll work closely with the cats' daily needs under staff supervision.
