Stay on the Reserve

Accommodation

Wake up to the sounds of the bushveld. Our self-catering guest house sits on the reserve itself, about 10 km outside Potchefstroom, with sable, kudu, gemsbok, impala and springbok on the property.

  • 3 bedrooms and 2 bathrooms, sleeping up to 6 guests (no children under 13).
  • Fully equipped kitchen, indoor braai and fireplace.
  • Free Wi-Fi, DStv, secure parking and load-shedding backup.
  • Book a feeding tour during your stay and meet the cats.

Before You Come

Visitor Guidelines

These rules protect our cats' welfare and their wild instincts, and keep everyone safe.

🚫

No Touching

We are strictly non-petting. Our cats are observed, never handled by visitors.

📅

Appointment Only

Please don't arrive unannounced. Booking keeps the camps calm and quiet.

🧭

Stay With Staff

All visits take place under staff supervision. Follow your guide's instructions at all times.

🤫

Keep It Calm

Quiet voices and slow movements help the cats behave naturally and stay relaxed.

Good to Know

Frequently Asked Questions

When do tours take place?

Monday to Saturday at 4:00 PM, at feeding time. There are no tours on Sundays because the cats fast one day a week, as they would in the wild.

Can I stay over?

Yes. Our self-catering guest house on the reserve sleeps up to 6 (no children under 13). Book it on LekkeSlaap or ask us a question.

How much does a tour cost?

R200 for adults (18+), R100 for children aged 6 to 18, and free for kids under 6.

Can I pet or touch the cats?

No. Barbosa K&K has a strict zero-exploitation, non-petting policy. This protects the cats' welfare and the wild behaviour they need for re-wilding.

How do I book?

Message us on WhatsApp (065 559 1257) or send an enquiry. All visits are by prior appointment only.

Where are you located?

Plot 17, Wilgeboom, Potchefstroom, 2531, North West Province. See the map on our contact page.
