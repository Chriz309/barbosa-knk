Moral Stewardship

Ethics First in Biological Conservation

At Barbosa K&K, biological ethics take precedence. Our core objective involves the preservation of rare predatory species through intentional propagation and unwavering focus on feline health.

We work to secure a viable future for the African cheetah, proving that human expertise and wild nature can thrive in mutual balance.

Direct Conservation Impact

  • Data-driven reproduction: Maximizing genetic diversity among endangered lineages.
  • Principled sanctuary: Comprehensive predator habitats and nutritional regimens.
  • Outreach & Education: Training the next generation of wildlife caretakers.

The Origin Story

Dr. Diana du Cros

A distinguished scientist in biology and protein biochemistry who redirected her life’s work toward the protection of vulnerable predators.

Transitioning from specialized international research laboratories across the United States and Australia, Dr. du Cros established operations in Potchefstroom with an unambiguous mandate: halt the trajectory toward cheetah extinction.

In 2016, she dedicated her personal resources to establish Barbosa K&K. Today, the facility serves as a benchmark for predator well-being, scientific breeding, and applied wildlife management.
