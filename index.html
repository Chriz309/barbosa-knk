Moral Stewardship

Ethics First in Biological Conservation

At Barbosa K&K, biological ethics take precedence. Our primary mandate is the intentional re-wilding of captive-bred cheetahs whose lineages will replenish protected African wilderness areas.

We combine advanced feline nutrition, data-driven reproductive genetics and expansive natural enclosures to ensure our cheetahs, caracals, servals and ocelots flourish.

  • Re-wilding focus: preparing offspring for release into wild habitat.
  • Zero-exploitation policy: strictly non-petting; respectful observation under staff supervision.
  • Diverse ecosystem: a working reserve supporting 8 game species alongside our predator families.

The Origin Story

Dr. Diana du Cros

A scientist in biology and protein biochemistry who redirected her life's work toward protecting vulnerable predators.

After her PhD research in Australian laboratories and a career as a police officer in the United States, Dr. du Cros set up in Potchefstroom with one clear mandate: halt the trajectory toward cheetah extinction. In 2016 she committed her personal resources to establishing Barbosa K&K.
