Moral Stewardship

Ethics First in Biological Conservation

At Barbosa K&K, biological ethics take precedence. Our primary mandate is the intentional re-wilding of captive-bred cheetahs whose lineages will replenish protected African wilderness areas.

We combine advanced feline nutrition, data-driven reproductive genetics, and expansive natural enclosures to ensure our cheetahs, caracals, servals, and ocelots flourish.

  • Re-wilding Focus: Preparing offspring for wild habitat release[cite: 2, 3].
  • Zero-Exploitation Policy: Strictly non-petting; respectful animal observation under staff supervision[cite: 2, 3].
  • Diverse Ecosystem: A functioning reserve supporting 8 game species alongside our predator families[cite: 2, 3].

The Origin Story

Dr. Diana du Cros

A distinguished scientist in biology and protein biochemistry who redirected her life's work toward the protection of vulnerable predators.

Transitioning from specialized international research laboratories across the United States and Australia, Dr. du Cros established operations in Potchefstroom with an unambiguous mandate: halt the trajectory toward cheetah extinction.

In 2016, she dedicated her personal resources to establish Barbosa K&K. Today, the facility serves as a benchmark for predator well-being, scientific breeding, and applied wildlife management.

Barbosa K&K Volunteer Programme

Live, learn, and contribute on the front lines. Every cent raised through our volunteer program is directly allocated to meat purchases, specialized veterinary diets, small cat habitats, and cheetah enclosure construction.

View Daily Working Routine:

Winter Daily Timetable

Feeding time begins promptly at 16:00
07:30 – 09:00 Morning enclosure rounds: clearing leftovers, collecting bowls, water inspection & butchery prep[cite: 2, 3].
09:00 – 09:30 Tea break and team regroup[cite: 2, 3].
09:30 – 13:00 Camp maintenance, game fence inspections, and invasive alien plant removal[cite: 2, 3].
13:00 – 14:00 Lunch break at the Guest House[cite: 2, 3].
14:00 – 17:00 Afternoon task assignments. Final meat prep begins; predator feeding takes place at 16:00[cite: 2, 3].

Summer Daily Timetable

Adjusted to avoid peak heat; cats eat at 17:00 when temperatures drop.
07:30 – 09:00 Early cool-morning enclosure cleaning, sanitizing bowls, and butchery prep[cite: 2, 3].
09:00 – 09:30 Tea break and shade rest[cite: 2, 3].
09:30 – 13:00 Field maintenance and reserve duties before midday heat[cite: 2, 3].
13:00 – 15:00 Extended midday heat break to stay rested and protected from hot summer sun.
15:00 – 18:00 Late afternoon work shift. Final feeding preparation starts; predator feeding takes place at 17:00.

* Note: Volunteers operate 7 days a week, with off-time on Saturdays from 12:00 to feeding time, and Sunday afternoons following morning rounds[cite: 2, 3].
